T'ai Chi For All

Instructional mobile application and movement platform.

Platform Purpose

T'ai Chi For All is an instructional cross-platform mobile application built to support the Lotus Academy through fundraising and accessible movement instruction. Featuring local device asset caching and structured form guides, it brings mindful movement directly to practitioners of all levels.

Development Status

This platform is currently undergoing active refinement and expansion to optimize 3D viewport rendering and instructional caching.
